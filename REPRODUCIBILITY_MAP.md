## Known reproducibility limits

- Original timestamps are unavailable for Samples 1-27, so onset/period
  sensitivity can be checked only for Samples 28-36 and the six fabric records.
- The exact ESP32 variant, ADC pin, acquisition-firmware source/version,
  fixed-resistor tolerance and any firmware-level voltage correction were not
  retained.
- No known-resistor or calibrated-multimeter comparison was retained, so
  absolute resistance error cannot be quantified retrospectively.
- Original PrusaSlicer projects and G-code were not retained. Perimeter count,
  extrusion width, flow multiplier, road-angle sequence, infill overlap,
  cooling and retraction settings are therefore unavailable and have not been
  reconstructed.
- Rows above that say a layout-only or one-off script was not retained are an
  explicit record of that limitation; frozen numerical inputs remain supplied.
